Hey, I'm Adarsh Jaiswal, a final-year Computer Science & Engineering student at Galgotias College of Engineering & Technology. I'd call myself a fairly passionate software developer — I like understanding how things work under the hood, not just getting them to run.

Most of my time goes into Java and Python, and I've been spending the last few months getting comfortable with Full Stack Development and the basics of Machine Learning — building small models, cleaning datasets, and figuring out how to actually deploy them instead of leaving everything in a notebook.

Alongside that, I keep practicing Data Structures & Algorithms regularly because I genuinely enjoy the problem-solving part of CS — there's something satisfying about getting a brute-force solution down to something efficient.

"First, solve the problem. Then, write the code."

I'm still figuring a lot of things out, and that's okay — I'm always trying to pick up new technologies and get a little better at this than I was last semester. Currently looking for internships where I can learn from people who've been doing this longer than me.
